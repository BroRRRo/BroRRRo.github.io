Before you plan your event

Do we need gaming experience?

Not for Casual Gaming. We agree the games and format around participants’ experience, and our administrators help everyone get started.

How many people can take part?

From 5 to 25 participants. The club has 20 Standard PCs and 5 VIP PCs. PC allocation is confirmed subject to availability.

What do the prices include?

Casual Gaming is €30 per person for 5 hours. Tournament is €40 per person, usually 3 hours, with duration agreed individually. Food is not included in the base price: add 1 drink and 1 DUO portion from our menu for an extra €20 per person in any event format, including Custom. All prices include VAT. Branding and prizes are quoted separately.

Can we customise our event?

Yes. Ask about Custom Events to agree games, activities, branding and special requests. Extras and feasibility are confirmed with the club.

Does a WhatsApp message confirm a booking?

No. The club needs to confirm availability, pricing and booking terms.
